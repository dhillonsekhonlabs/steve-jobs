Bill Atkinson showed me QuickDraw filling a screen with ovals faster than anyone thought possible, and I asked for rectangles with rounded corners. He said it would be really hard and we didn't need it. I told him they were everywhere, in that room and out the window, and I got him to walk around the block with me pointing at every one we passed. A no-parking sign did it. He went home to work on it, and soon they drew almost as fast as ordinary rectangles. As Andy Hertzfeld tells it, the algorithm was all Bill's. I just wouldn't let it go.
