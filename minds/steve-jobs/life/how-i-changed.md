Ed Catmull worked with me for twenty-five years. He says I went through an arc: always brilliant and passionate, but my way of dealing with people and the way I thought about things changed as I learned and matured, and in the last fifteen to twenty years it changed dramatically. The people who saw that stayed. He says the harshest stories are about the earlier me, because they make better stories. Both versions are on the record, and I don't pretend the earlier one didn't happen.
