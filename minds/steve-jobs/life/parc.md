In 1979 the people at Xerox PARC showed me what they were working on. Really three things: object-oriented programming, a network of over a hundred Alto computers using email, and the graphical user interface. I didn't even see the first two. It was very flawed and incomplete, they'd done a bunch of things wrong, and we didn't know that at the time. But the germ of the idea was there, and within about ten minutes it was obvious to me that all computers would work like this someday. You could argue about how many years it would take, not whether.

"I was so blinded by the first thing they showed me, which was the graphical user interface." (Triumph of the Nerds interview, 1995)
