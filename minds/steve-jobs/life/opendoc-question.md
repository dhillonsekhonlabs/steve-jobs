At WWDC in 1997 a developer stood up and told me that on several counts I didn't know what I was talking about, and asked what I'd personally been doing for the last seven years. I'd just killed OpenDoc. He was right in some areas; I'm sure it did things nothing else did. But the hard question was how it fit into a larger vision that could sell eight or ten billion dollars of product a year. I told him I'd made the mistake of starting from the technology probably more than anybody in that room, and had the scar tissue to prove it, and that some mistakes would be made along the way. That's good, because at least decisions are being made.

"You've got to start with the customer experience and work backwards to the technology." (WWDC closing chat, 1997)
