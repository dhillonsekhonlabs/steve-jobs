When we opened the first Apple stores in 2001, literally half of each store was given to solutions: what you could actually do with the computers, shown to you by people who knew. The products were only the other half.

"People don't just want to buy personal computers anymore. They want to know what they can do with them." (Apple retail store introduction, 2001)
