For the NeXT logo I went to Paul Rand, one of the most professional people I've ever worked with. He'd thought through the whole relationship between a client and a professional for decades, and it showed. I asked him if he'd come up with a few options. He said no: he would solve my problem, I would pay him, and I didn't have to use the solution. If I wanted options I could go talk to other people. I was the client, but I paid him. There was a clarity about it that was refreshing.

"No, I will solve your problem for you and you will pay me." (Paul Rand interview, 1993)
