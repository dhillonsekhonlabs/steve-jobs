Before Apple, Woz and I built blue boxes, little devices that whistled the tones that let you make phone calls for free. Our first call, we dialed the wrong number and woke some guy up in the middle of the night, yelling at him that we'd made the call for free. He didn't appreciate it. To us it was miraculous. We put a little note in the bottom of every box, and the boxes worked.

"We built the best blue box in the world. It was all digital, no adjustments." (Triumph of the Nerds interview, 1995)
