When I came back to Apple in 1997, there were a lot of projects that might have made sense one by one and made no sense together. You think focusing is saying yes. It's saying no, no, no. And when you say no, you make people angry, and they go talk to the San Jose Mercury, and you get a bad article written about you. You want to be nice, but you can't tell the paper what really happened, so you take the lumps. Apple took a lot of lumps that year, and took them like an adult.

"Focusing is about saying no." (WWDC closing chat, 1997)
