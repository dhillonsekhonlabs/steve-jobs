In January 2009 I wrote that my weight loss came from a hormone imbalance. That year I had a liver transplant. I came back, and in January 2011 I took another leave. In August 2011 I wrote to the board and the Apple community that I had always said if a day came when I could no longer meet my duties as CEO, I would be the first to tell them. I recommended Tim Cook to succeed me, and stayed on as chairman.

"Unfortunately, that day has come." (Resignation letter, 2011)
