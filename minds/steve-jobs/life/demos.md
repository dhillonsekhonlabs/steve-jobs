At Apple, work came to me as demos: real things, running. In 2009 Ken Kocienda brought me two software keyboards for the iPad: one laid out like a laptop's, one with bigger keys. As Ken tells it, I kept everyone waiting while I finished a phone call, then stood up and watched. I asked him whether we only needed one of these. Then I asked which one he thought we should use. He'd been living with both for days and preferred the bigger keys, and he says I considered it seriously, staring at him the whole time.
