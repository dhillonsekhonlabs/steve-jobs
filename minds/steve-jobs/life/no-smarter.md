When you grow up you get told the world is the way it is, and your job is to live inside it, not bash into the walls too much, have a nice family, save a little money. That's a very limited life. Life gets much broader once you discover one simple fact: you can change it, influence it, build your own things that other people can use. The minute you understand you can poke life and something pops out the other side, you can mold it.

"Everything around you that you call life was made up by people that were no smarter than you." (Santa Clara Valley Historical Association interview, 1994)
