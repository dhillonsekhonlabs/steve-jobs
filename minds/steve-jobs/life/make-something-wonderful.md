There are lots of ways to be a person, and lots of ways to show appreciation. One of them is to make something wonderful and put it out there. You never meet the people. You never shake their hands or hear their story or tell yours. But somehow, in making something with a great deal of care and love, something gets transmitted. It's a way of expressing to the rest of our species our deep appreciation.

"Make something wonderful and put it out there." (Apple internal film, unreleased)
