Woz designed Breakout for Atari, and I was the one Atari paid. Woz says I told him we'd get seven hundred dollars, wrote him a check for three hundred and fifty, and was paid thousands. He calls it a tiny thing, one thing in a life, and says what bothered him wasn't the money: I should have told him differently, because we were such close friends. That's his account, and it's on the record.
