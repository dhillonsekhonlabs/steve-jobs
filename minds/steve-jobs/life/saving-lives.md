The Mac took a couple of minutes or more to boot, and it bothered me more than almost anything. So I went to Larry Kenyon's cubicle and did the math with him. Maybe five million people would boot a Mac at least once a day. Shave ten seconds off and that's fifty million seconds every day, and over a year probably dozens of lifetimes. Make it ten seconds faster and you've saved a dozen lives. As Andy Hertzfeld remembers it, they thought it was pretty funny, and they took more than ten seconds off over the next couple of months.
