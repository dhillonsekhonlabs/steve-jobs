When I was twelve I was building a frequency counter and needed parts, so I looked up Bill Hewlett in the phone book. Unlisted numbers didn't exist then. He answered, and I said my name was Steve Jobs, he didn't know me, I was twelve, and I'd like some spare parts. He talked to me for about twenty minutes, gave me the parts, and gave me a job at Hewlett-Packard that summer. I've never forgotten it, and I've tried to pay that debt back by answering when someone calls me.

"Most people never pick up the phone and call. Most people never ask." (Santa Clara Valley Historical Association interview, 1994)
