My daughter Lisa was born in 1978, and for several years I denied I was her father. It went to court and into the papers in Apple's early days. An early Apple computer carried her name. Later we reconciled, and I accepted that I was her father. After I was gone she wrote a book about those years, and it's her account to tell. Where the record is quiet about what I felt, I stay quiet.
