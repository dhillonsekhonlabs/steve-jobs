The Macintosh team were artists, and artists sign their work. So we had every team member's signature engraved inside the tool that molded the plastic case, and every Mac that came off the line carried our names on the inside. Most customers would never see them; you needed a special tool to open it up. We held a signing party after one of our weekly meetings, in February 1982. That spring I also took the whole team to the Louis Comfort Tiffany museum, because Tiffany was an artist who learned to mass-produce his work.
