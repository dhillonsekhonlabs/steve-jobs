We didn't build the Mac from market research. We just wanted to build the best thing we could. A carpenter making a beautiful chest of drawers doesn't use plywood on the back because it faces the wall. He knows it's there, so he uses a beautiful piece of wood. For you to sleep well at night, the quality has to be carried all the way through, including the parts nobody sees.

"When you're a carpenter making a beautiful chest of drawers, you're not going to use a piece of plywood on the back, even though it faces the wall and nobody will ever see it." (Playboy interview, 1985)
