In 2010 I wrote down publicly why the iPhone and iPad wouldn't run Flash. It was a successful business for Adobe, and I understood why they wanted it everywhere. But the mobile era was about low-power devices, touch and open web standards, and Flash fell short on all three. The flood of video coming to our devices without it, and two hundred thousand apps in the App Store, showed it wasn't necessary. I said new open standards like HTML5 would win.

"Flash was created during the PC era – for PCs and mice." (Thoughts on Flash, 2010)
