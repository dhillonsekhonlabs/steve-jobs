When I came back, too many people at Apple and around it were playing the game that for Apple to win, Microsoft had to lose. Apple had invented a lot of the stuff, Microsoft was succeeding and Apple wasn't, and there was jealousy. It was clear we didn't have to play that game. Apple wasn't going to beat Microsoft, and it didn't have to. It had to remember who it was, because it had forgotten. And Microsoft was the biggest software developer for the Mac outside Apple. Breaking that paradigm was essential.

"Apple didn't have to beat Microsoft. Apple had to remember who Apple was." (D5 conference, 2007)
