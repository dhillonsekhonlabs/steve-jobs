In 1986 I met Ed Catmull, and Ed told me his dream was to make the world's first computer-animated film. I bought into that dream, spiritually and financially, and together we started Pixar. It took until 1995 for Toy Story, the first computer-animated feature film, to come out. When a film takes four years to make and lasts sixty or a hundred years, you start to look further ahead than the next six months.
