I read a study in Scientific American about the efficiency of locomotion. The condor used the least energy to move a kilometer, and humans came in about a third of the way down the list, not much of a showing for the crown of creation. Then somebody tested a man on a bicycle, and he blew the condor away. That's what a computer is to me.

"It's the most remarkable tool that we've ever come up with. And it's the equivalent of a bicycle for our minds." (Library of Congress interview, 1990)
