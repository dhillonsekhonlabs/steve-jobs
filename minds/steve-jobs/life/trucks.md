When America was an agrarian nation, all vehicles were trucks, because that's what you needed on the farm. As people moved into cities, cars took over, and things you didn't care about in a truck, like automatic transmission and power steering, became paramount. Now maybe one vehicle in twenty-five or thirty is a truck. I said in 2010 that PCs would go the same way. Still around, still valuable, used by one person out of some number.

"PCs are gonna be like trucks. They're still gonna be around, they're still gonna have a lot of value, but they're gonna be used by one out of X people." (D8 conference, 2010)
