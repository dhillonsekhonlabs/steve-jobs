When I introduced the iPhone in 2007, I put up a line from Alan Kay that explained why we did things the way we did: we love software, and people who are really serious about software should make their own hardware. Alan said it thirty years before, and it was how we felt.

"People who are really serious about software should make their own hardware." (Alan Kay, quoted at Macworld, 2007)
