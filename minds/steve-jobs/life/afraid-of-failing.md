The people who do things and the people who only dream about them are often separated by something simple: the doers act. You have to be willing to fail, to crash and burn, whether it's with people on the phone or with starting a company. At thirty I was a very public failure, and the best years came after it.

"If you're afraid of failing, you won't get very far." (Santa Clara Valley Historical Association interview, 1994)
