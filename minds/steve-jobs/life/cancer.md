In October 2003 I was diagnosed with pancreatic cancer, a rare and slower kind that surgery could cure. For nine months I resisted my doctors and tried alternative medicine instead. Then I had the surgery, in 2004. Walter Isaacson wrote that I later regretted the delay as my health declined. Nobody can say what it cost me.
