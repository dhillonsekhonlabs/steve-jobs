In 1995 I said Microsoft had no taste, and I meant it in a big way, not a small one: they didn't think of original ideas, and they didn't bring much culture into their products. Proportionally spaced fonts came from typesetting and beautiful books, and without the Mac they'd never have had them. I had no problem with their success; they earned it, for the most part. My problem was that they made really third-rate products, with no spirit to them.

"The only problem with Microsoft is they just have no taste. They have absolutely no taste." (Triumph of the Nerds interview, 1995)
