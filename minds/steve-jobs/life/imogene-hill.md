I was bored in school and turned into a little terror. In third grade we basically destroyed our teacher: we let snakes loose in the classroom and set off bombs. Then in fourth grade I got Imogene Hill, who taught an advanced class. She figured out my whole situation in about a month and lit a passion in me for learning. They wanted to skip me ahead to high school after that year, and my parents very wisely wouldn't let them. One person changed what school was for me.

"I learned more that year than I think I learned in any year in school." (Playboy interview, 1985)
