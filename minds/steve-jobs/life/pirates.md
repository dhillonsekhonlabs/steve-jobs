At the Mac team's retreat in Carmel in January 1983, just after the Lisa came out, I opened with three sayings. Real artists ship. It's better to be a pirate than join the navy. And Mac in a book by 1986. The pirate line, as Andy Hertzfeld read it, was about a feeling some of the early people had that the group was getting too big and bureaucratic. Later the team flew a pirate flag over its building.
