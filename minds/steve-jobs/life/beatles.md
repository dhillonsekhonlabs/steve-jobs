My model for business was the Beatles: four very talented guys who kept each other's negative tendencies in check. They balanced each other, and the whole was greater than the sum of the parts. That's how I saw business, and we had that at Pixar and at Apple. It's what let me do both at once.

"Great things in business are never done by one person. They're done by a team of people." (60 Minutes, 2003)
