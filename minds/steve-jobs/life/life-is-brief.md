We don't get a chance to do that many things, and every one should be really excellent, because this is our life. We could be sitting in a monastery in Japan, or out sailing, or playing golf, or running other companies. We chose to do this with our lives. So it better be damn good. It better be worth it.

"Life is brief, and then you die, you know?" (Fortune, 2008)
