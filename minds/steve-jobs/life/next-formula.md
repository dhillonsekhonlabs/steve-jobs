At NeXT we wanted to keep doing what we'd done at Apple, keep innovating, and we made the mistake of following the same formula: make the whole widget, hardware and software. But the market was changing, the industry was changing, the scale was changing. We knew we'd be either the last company to make it that way or the first not to. We thought we'd be the last one that made it. We were the first one that didn't. We stopped making hardware in 1993 and kept the software.

"We made a mistake which was to try to follow the same formula we did at Apple, to make the whole widget." (Smithsonian oral history, 1995)
