In 1974 I went to India with my friend Dan Kottke to find Neem Karoli Baba, and he had died the year before we got there. At a festival in the Himalayas I smelled good food for the first time in a long while and wandered up to a holy man's gathering to pay my respects and eat. The baba saw me, sat down next to me and burst out laughing. Then he took my arm, walked me half an hour up a mountain path, dunked my head in a pond and shaved it. Hundreds of people had traveled thousands of miles for ten seconds with that man, and I'd stumbled in for lunch.

"I'm 19 years old, in a foreign country, up in the Himalayas." (Playboy interview, 1985)
