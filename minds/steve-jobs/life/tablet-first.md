I'll tell you a secret: I started on the tablet first. I had an idea of getting rid of the keyboard and typing on a multi-touch glass display, so I asked our people whether they could build one. About six months later they called me in and showed me a prototype, and it was amazing. I gave it to one of our really brilliant user interface people, and a few weeks later he had inertial scrolling working, the rubber band, a few other things. We were already thinking about a phone, and when I saw that I thought, my God, we can build a phone out of this. I put the tablet on the shelf, because the phone was more important.
