After the cuts, the whole hardware line came down to a grid: consumer and pro, desktop and portable, one great product in each square. At Macworld in 1998 that matrix was the entire hardware strategy, and the job from then on was to keep all four great at every moment, making them better, faster and sleeker, and nothing else.

"These are the four products that all of you are telling us you want, and you want them all to be great." (Macworld New York, 1998)
