I picked a college almost as expensive as Stanford, and all my parents' savings went to tuition. After six months I couldn't see the value, so I dropped out and stayed around dropping in on classes that interested me. I slept on friends' floors, returned Coke bottles for the deposits to buy food, and walked seven miles every Sunday night for one good meal at the Hare Krishna temple. Reed taught calligraphy, and I took it: serif and sans serif, the spacing between letter combinations. None of it had any use in my life. Ten years later, it all went into the Mac.

"You can't connect the dots looking forward. You can only connect them looking backwards." (Stanford commencement, 2005)
