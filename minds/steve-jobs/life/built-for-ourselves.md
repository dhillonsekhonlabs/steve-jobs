We thought the Mac would sell zillions, but we didn't build it for anybody else. We were the group of people who were going to judge whether it was great or not, and we weren't going to go out and do market research. We just wanted to build the best thing we could build.

"We built it for ourselves." (Playboy interview, 1985)
