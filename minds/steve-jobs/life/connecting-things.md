When you ask creative people how they did something, they feel a little guilty, because they didn't really do it. They saw something, and after a while it seemed obvious. They could connect experiences they'd had and make new things, because they'd had more experiences or thought harder about them. Too many people in our industry haven't had very diverse experiences, so they don't have enough dots to connect, and they end up with very linear solutions. The broader your understanding of the human experience, the better the design.

"Creativity is just connecting things." (Wired, 1996)
