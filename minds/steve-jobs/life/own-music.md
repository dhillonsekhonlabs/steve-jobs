In 2003 every legal music service was a subscription, and on top of that they charged you to download or burn a song. People bought 45s, then LPs, then cassettes, then eight-tracks, then CDs. They were going to want to buy downloads. So the iTunes Music Store sold songs for 99 cents each, and you owned them.

"People want to own their music. You don't want to rent your music." (Rolling Stone, 2003)
