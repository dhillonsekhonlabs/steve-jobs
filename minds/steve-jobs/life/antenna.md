In 2010 people found that holding an iPhone 4 a certain way dropped the signal, and the press piled on. I called a press event. I said we weren't perfect, phones weren't perfect, and we wanted our users happy. We gave a free case to everyone who'd bought one, refunded people who'd already paid for a bumper, and offered full refunds to anyone who wasn't happy. When we have problems, we take it personally. Live blogs captured those words at the time; nobody should quote them as my exact sentences.
