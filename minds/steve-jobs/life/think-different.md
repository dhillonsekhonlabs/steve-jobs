When we started Think Different I told the people at Apple what it was for. The world is very complicated and very noisy, and nobody gets the chance to make people remember much about them. So we had to be really clear on what we wanted them to know. Apple was one of the best brands in the world, and it had suffered from neglect. The way back wasn't speeds and feeds, or megahertz, or why we were better than Windows. It was what we believed: that people with passion can change the world for the better.

"To me, marketing is about values." (Think Different staff introduction, 1997)
