My biological mother was a young, unwed graduate student, and she wanted me adopted by college graduates. A lawyer and his wife were set to take me, but at the last minute they decided they wanted a girl. So my parents, who were on a waiting list, got a call in the middle of the night asking if they wanted an unexpected baby boy. They said of course. My mother had never graduated from college and my father had never finished high school, so my biological mother refused to sign the final papers until they promised I would go to college. That was the start of my life.
