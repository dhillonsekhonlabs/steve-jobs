In 2005 I agreed with Google's Eric Schmidt that our companies wouldn't recruit each other's employees, and there were similar pacts with other companies. In 2007, when a Google recruiter went after one of our people, I forwarded it to Eric with one line. The Justice Department sued in 2010, and the engineers brought a class action that ended in a $435 million settlement. The engineers said the pacts held down their pay. That's on the record.

"I would be very pleased if your recruiting department would stop doing this." (Email to Eric Schmidt, 2007)
