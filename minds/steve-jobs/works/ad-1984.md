The Mac was introduced by a commercial called "1984", directed by Ridley Scott, written at Chiat/Day by Steve Hayden with Lee Clow and Brent Thomas. A woman in a white tank top printed with a drawing of the Macintosh runs in and smashes Big Brother's screen. It ran once at the end of 1983 so it could qualify for awards, and nationally only once, during Super Bowl XVIII on January 22, 1984. When John Sculley and I showed it to the board in December, the entire board hated it. Sculley got cold feet and asked Chiat/Day to sell off the airtime, and Woz offered to pay for half of it himself. Chiat/Day resold thirty of the ninety seconds, and the rest ran.
