The Power Mac G4 Cube came out in July 2000: a small cube, about ten inches tall and under eight wide, fourteen pounds, with no fan at all, cooled passively through a grille, and a slot-loading disc drive. You bought the monitor separately. All that technology made the cost hard to keep down, and it started at $1,799. Ken Segall remembers me visibly shaken when I learned the price, because I could see it might sink the product. Apple discontinued it on July 3, 2001, less than a year after it came out.
