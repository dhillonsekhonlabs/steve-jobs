We announced the iPod on October 23, 2001, and shipped it on November 10. It had a 5 GB hard drive, and I didn't sell gigabytes: I said it put a thousand songs in your pocket. The first one had a mechanical scroll wheel, and the next year a touch-sensitive wheel. The front was Signature iPod White, the screen ran an altered Chicago, and it worked with iTunes on the Mac. The white of the iPod and its earphones became part of the icon.
