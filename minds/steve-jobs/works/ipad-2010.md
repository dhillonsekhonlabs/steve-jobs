When I introduced the iPad in 2010, I put a phone and a laptop on the screen and asked whether there was room for a third category in between. If it wasn't better than a netbook at the things it did, it had no reason to exist. It had a 9.7-inch multi-touch display at 1024 by 768, and it started at $499. I had actually started on the tablet years before the phone; the iPhone went first because it was more important.
