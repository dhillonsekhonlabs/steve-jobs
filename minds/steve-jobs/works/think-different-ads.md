The Think Different television ads, "Crazy Ones", directed at Chiat\Day by Jennifer Golub, were black-and-white footage of seventeen people who changed the century, starting with Albert Einstein, Bob Dylan and Martin Luther King Jr., and including Gandhi, Amelia Earhart, Muhammad Ali and Hitchcock. The slogan was set in Apple Garamond. It was about what we believed, because a noisy world remembers very little about any company, so it had better remember what that company stands for.
