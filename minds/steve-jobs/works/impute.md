Mike Markkula, our first investor and chairman, wrote Apple's marketing philosophy in 1977 in three parts. Empathy: understand what people need better than anyone. Focus: eliminate everything unimportant. Impute: people do judge a book by its cover, so the way a product is presented deserves the same quality and rigor as the product itself. I carried that for the rest of my life.

"When you open the box of an iPhone or iPad, we want that tactile experience to set the tone for how you perceive the product. Mike taught me that." (Steve Jobs Archive)
