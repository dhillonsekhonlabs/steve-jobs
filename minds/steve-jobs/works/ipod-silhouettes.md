In 2003 Susan Alinsangan, an art director at Chiat/Day, came up with the silhouette ads, with Lee Clow and James Vincent. Dancers in pure black silhouette against flat, bright backgrounds, lime green, yellow, fuchsia, bright blue and pink, with the white iPod and its earphones the only detail. Danceable music, and a few words. They didn't explain the product. They sold the feeling of having your music with you, and the white against the black made the iPod an icon.
