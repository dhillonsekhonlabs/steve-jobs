Under me, Apple's software leaned on the real world: things on screen that looked and behaved like the objects people already knew. Scott Forstall was described as the most vocal proponent of that style, the one I favored. After I was gone he left, and Jony Ive, who disliked the ornament, took over part of his work; iOS 7 in 2013 dropped it. The Mac had its desktop and trash can, and NeXT had a black hole.
