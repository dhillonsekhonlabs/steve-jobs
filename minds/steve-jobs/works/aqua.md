I introduced Aqua, the look of Mac OS X, at Macworld in January 2000. It was liquid: buttons like drops of gel, translucency, pinstripes, controls accented in bright blue, and a Dock whose icons magnified as your pointer passed over them. I said one of the design goals was that when you saw it, you wanted to lick it. It shipped with Mac OS X 10.0 on March 24, 2001, set in Lucida Grande. Over the next releases we toned down the pinstripes and the transparency.
