For the iMac after the first one, Jony's team had a flat screen with the computer stuck onto its back, and I hated it. It lacked purity. Why have a flat display if you're going to glom all that stuff on its back? As Walter Isaacson tells it, I said we should let each element be true to itself. The shape that came out of it was inspired by a sunflower: the computer in a hemispheric base, the screen floating on a single stainless steel arm. It took two years. We dropped the colorful translucency for opaque white with silver accents.
