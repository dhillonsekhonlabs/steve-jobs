NeXT's computer was a cube, one foot on each side, in die-cast magnesium. On stage in October 1988 I held up the whole system on a single board one foot square. On screen, there was no trash can: you dragged a file into a black hole and it went off into hyperspace. Paul Rand's logo was a simple black box that broke the company name onto two lines, and it won me over. As a business, the whole machine was the wrong formula.

"A one foot square board containing the entire system." (NeXT introduction, 1988)
