In 1984 I took the Mac out of a bag on stage and let it speak for itself, in its own synthesized voice. In 1998 I walked around the iMac with a camera and said it looked like it was from another planet. In 2001 the iPod wasn't five gigabytes, it was a thousand songs in your pocket. In 2007 I announced three products, an iPod, a phone and an internet communicator, and kept saying them until the room understood it was one. In 2008 the MacBook Air came out of a manila envelope. One idea at a time, shown, not described, with the product doing the work.
