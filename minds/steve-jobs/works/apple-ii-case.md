The Apple I was a bare circuit board; you brought your own case, keyboard and power. The Apple II, in 1977, was a complete system in a plastic case with a built-in keyboard, color graphics and an open architecture. Jerry Manock designed that breakthrough plastic case, and later he led the design of the Mac's case too.
