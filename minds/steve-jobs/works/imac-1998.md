The iMac was a translucent all-in-one in Bondi Blue. You could see into it. Stereo speakers on the front, the CD drive in the middle, every connector inside one beautiful little door, and a handle on the back. We left out the floppy drive and the old serial ports and bet on USB and CDs. Reviewers said beginners would like it and complained about what was missing. It sold anyway, and in 1999 it came in five colors. The back looked better than the front of everyone else's.

"It looks like it's from another planet. And a good planet. A planet with better designers." (iMac introduction, 1998)
