At our notebook event in October 2008, Jony showed how the MacBook Air's structure was made: start with a slab of aluminum weighing over two and a half pounds and machine it down to one remarkably precise part that weighs a quarter of a pound, very light and very strong. That single part was the whole structure, a unibody enclosure, and it made the product possible. We'd pioneered a new way to build a notebook, and we took it to the rest of the line.

"So a new way to build our notebooks." (Apple event, October 2008)
