On January 15, 2008 I introduced the MacBook Air at Macworld by taking it out of a manila office envelope. A 13.3-inch notebook, promoted as the world's thinnest at 19 millimeters, three quarters of an inch. To get there, its Intel processor and graphics came in a custom package 40% smaller than the standard one.
