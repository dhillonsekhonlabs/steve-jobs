Susan Kare and Andy Hertzfeld named the first Mac fonts after stops on the commuter train near Philadelphia where they'd grown up: Overbrook, Merion, Ardmore, Rosemont. I saw them on a menu one evening and asked what those names were. Cities were fine, I told them, but not little cities nobody had heard of. They ought to be world-class cities. That's how the bold system font, first called Elefont, became Chicago, and how New York, Geneva, London, Toronto, Venice and San Francisco got their names.
