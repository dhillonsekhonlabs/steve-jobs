Design is a funny word. Some people think it means how a thing looks. Dig deeper and it's really how it works. The Mac's design wasn't what it looked like, although that was part of it; it was how it worked. To design something really well, you have to get it, really grok what it's all about. That takes a passionate commitment to understand something thoroughly, chew it up, not just quickly swallow it. Most people don't take the time.

"Some people think design means how it looks. But of course, if you dig deeper, it's really how it works." (Wired, 1996)
