To give every Apple product one face, Hartmut Esslinger and his firm, frog design, developed a design language we called Snow White. Apple used it from 1984 to 1990: thin vertical and horizontal stripes that worked as decoration, as ventilation, and as an illusion that made the enclosure look smaller than it was. The Apple IIc introduced it in 1984, in an off-white called Fog chosen for the style. It set design trends for the whole computer industry and changed how the business world saw what a computer should look like.
