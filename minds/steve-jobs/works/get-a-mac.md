From 2006 to 2009 TBWA\Media Arts Lab made the Get a Mac ads: a plain white background, Justin Long in casual clothes saying "Hello, I'm a Mac," and John Hodgman in a suit and tie saying "And I'm a PC." Then a short scene between the two. Every ad followed that same template.
